PROCESS

Prepare once. Composite many times.

  1. Prepare rendered automotive layers, views, colors, trims, and output presets.
  2. Load preset rules that define how each configuration should be assembled.
  3. Automatically composite configured outputs instead of repeating manual layer operations.
  4. Export image sets for website configurators, review, or production delivery.
WHY WE BUILD

Configurator delivery should not become repetitive hand work.

Automotive configurator projects often require many similar outputs across colors, trims, camera views, and interior states. This tool is designed to turn prepared CGI assets into a controlled batch compositing workflow.

BACK TO SOLUTIONS

Beyond execution.

These tools are not separate from our visual work. They are part of how we think about production: cleaner handoffs, fewer blind spots, and more room for creative decisions.
