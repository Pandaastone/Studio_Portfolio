WORK ABOUT COURSE CONTACT
AUDI CONCEPT
AUTOMOTIVE / VISUALIZATION
CLIENT
Audi China
YEAR
2023
ROLE
Art Direction, 3D Design

This project explores the future of automotive visualization through a minimalist lens. The goal was to highlight the sleek lines and aerodynamic profile of the Audi concept vehicle using high-contrast lighting and atmospheric environments.

We utilized Redshift for rendering to achieve photorealistic results while maintaining artistic control over the lighting mood. The animation focuses on the interaction between light and the car's metallic surfaces.
